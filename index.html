About Us

01

BDAE Consulting is a building consulting firm dedicated to working together with our clients in due diligence, EPCM service, and carbon neutral services. We value creative solutions to provide critical insights and practical solutions.

With a growing team of diverse, experienced and values-driven specialists, our listen-first philosophy provides companies of every size and industry with effective solutions that align with their culture and objectives.

Headquartered in Shanghai, with offices in Shenyang and Singapore, our projects span across China, Thailand, Japan, and Southeast Asia.
